# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Epyatis MCP Central community welcoming, safe and constructive for everyone participating in good faith.

## Expected behavior

- Engage honestly, respectfully and with technical substance.
- Welcome different levels of experience and explain decisions without condescension.
- Give and receive constructive feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas, evidence and code rather than people.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality and clearly expressed boundaries.
- Credit sources and take responsibility for contributions.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination or sexualized conduct.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information or credentials without permission.
- Impersonation, deliberate deception or evasion of moderation.
- Commercial promotion unrelated to the purpose of the community.
- Any conduct that reasonably threatens the safety or well-being of participants.

## Scope

This Code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and other official project spaces, and when someone is officially representing the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not open a public issue for a sensitive conduct report. Contact the maintainer using the private contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile and identify the report as a Code of Conduct matter.

Reports will be reviewed as confidentially and impartially as practical. Depending on severity and pattern, maintainers may provide a warning, limit participation temporarily, remove content or permanently restrict access. Material moderation decisions should be explained to affected participants when doing so is safe and appropriate.
